Michigan Tech Preview Day 2023

Time: March 25, 2023

Location: Secure and Privacy (SnP) lab @Rekhi 318

 

Three volunteers from the Security and Privacy Lab were involved in the MTU Preview Day 2023: Harsh Singh (PhD student), Ryan Klemm (CS Undergraduate), Josh Dafoe (CS Undergraduate). The volunteers showed different demos on the on-going or past security and privacy projects of SnP lab, delivering cybersecurity knowledge to the potential incoming students. Various demos about cyber-physical system security, steganography, secure data recovery from malware attacks have been shown by the volunteers.
