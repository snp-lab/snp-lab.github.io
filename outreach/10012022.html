Michigan Tech Open House Showcase Fall 2022

Time: Oct 1, 2022

Location: Secure and Privacy (SnP) lab @Rekhi 318

 

The Michigan Tech Open House Showcase is the large information and resource fair in the Upper Peninsula of Michigan that provides prospective students and their families with the opportunity to explore a variety of departments and make a personal connection with faculty, staff, and students. 2 volunteers (Harsh Singh, Aditya Patil) from the SnP lab were involved in this year’s event. The volunteers showed the testbed of our smart vehicle research.
