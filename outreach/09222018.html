Michigan Tech Open House Showcase Fall 2018

Time: Sept 22, 2018

Location: Secure and Privacy lab @Rekhi 318

 

The Michigan Tech Open House Showcase is the large information and resource fair in the Upper Peninsula of Michigan that provides prospective students and their families with the opportunity to explore a variety of departments and make a personal connection with faculty, staff, and students. 4 volunteers were involved in this event, representing Security and Privacy (SnP) lab: Bo Chen (faculty@CS, director of SnP lab), Niusen Chen (PhD student), Ali Jalooli (PhD student), Manu Nandan Chemudupati (MS student). The volunteers showed different demos on the on-going or past security and privacy projects of SnP lab, delivering cybersecurity knowledge to both students and parents, raising awareness on data security, phishing attacks, etc.
